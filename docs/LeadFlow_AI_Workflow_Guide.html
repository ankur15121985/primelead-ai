1
Add a New Lead
  • 1 Navigate to Leads in the sidebar
  • 2 Click "Add Lead" button (top-right)
  • 3 Fill in customer details: Name (required), Phone, Email, Company
  • 4 Select Source (Website, WhatsApp, Facebook, etc.)
  • 5 Set Priority and Expected Value
  • 6 Click "Create Lead"
2
What Happens Automatically
  • ✓ Duplicate Detection: System checks for existing leads with same phone/email
  • ✓ Lead Scoring: Automatic 0-100 score based on priority, value, and contact completeness
  • ✓ Auto-Assignment: Lead assigned to least-loaded salesperson
  • ✓ Notification: Salesperson receives instant notification
  • ✓ Activity Log: Creation logged in lead timeline
3
Import from CSV
  • 1 Click "Import" on the Leads page
  • 2 Prepare CSV with columns: Name, Phone, Email, Company, Expected Value, Notes
  • 3 Upload the file
  • 4 System auto-skips duplicates and shows import count
4
Bulk Operations
  • 1 Select multiple leads using checkboxes
  • 2 Use bulk toolbar to Assign, Change Status, Add Tag, or Delete
  • 3 Changes apply to all selected leads at once
1
Sales Pipeline - Drag & Drop
  • 1 Navigate to Pipeline in the sidebar
  • 2 View all leads organized by stage columns
  • 3 Drag a lead card from one column
  • 4 Drop it into the next stage column
  • 5 Move is automatically logged in timeline and reflected in reports

1
Schedule a Follow-up
  • 1 Open any Lead Detail page
  • 2 Click "Schedule Follow-up"
  • 3 Choose type: Call / WhatsApp / Email / Meeting / Task
  • 4 Set Date & Time
  • 5 Add Notes about what to discuss
  • 6 Click "Schedule"
2
Complete a Follow-up
  • 1 Open Follow-ups page
  • 2 Find the task in Today or Overdue view
  • 3 Click the circular checkbox next to it
  • 4 Task moves to Completed view
3
Follow-up Views
  • 📅 Today: Follow-ups due today (high priority)
  • 🔴 Overdue: Past-due items (act now!)
  • 📆 Upcoming: Next few days (plan ahead)
  • ❌ Missed: Auto-detected lapsed tasks
1
Create GST Quotation
  • 1 Navigate to Quotations → Click "New Quotation"
  • 2 Add customer details: Name, Company, GSTIN, Email, Phone
  • 3 Add line items with Description, Quantity, Rate, Discount %, Tax %
  • 4 Select GST Type: CGST+SGST (same state) or IGST (inter-state)
  • 5 Set Validity Days and Payment Terms
  • 6 Click "Create Quotation" — Auto-numbered QT-2026-0001
  • 7 Download PDF and send to customer
  • 8 Mark as Sent, then Accepted when customer agrees
  • 9 Click "Convert to Invoice" when ready to bill

Super-Admin Panel Guide

Platform management for website handlers and administrators

1
Access Admin Panel
  • 1 Add your email to SUPER_ADMIN_EMAILS in server/.env
  • 2 Log in to LeadFlow AI normally
  • 3 Click your avatar → "Admin Panel"
  • 4 Or navigate directly to /admin
2
Admin Overview
  • 📊 Platform Totals: Organizations, Users, Leads, Revenue
  • 🏢 Top Organizations: Ranked by leads and revenue
  • 🆕 Recent Signups: Latest registered businesses
  • ⚠️ Error Feed: Recent system errors (last 50)
3
Manage Organizations
  • 1 Navigate to Organizations in admin sidebar
  • 2 View all registered businesses with stats
  • 3 Click org name for detailed view
  • 4 Suspend: Temporarily disable org (users can't log in)
  • 5 Re-activate: Restore access
  • 6 Change Plan: Switch between Starter/Growth/Business
4
User Management
  • 1 Navigate to Users in admin sidebar
  • 2 View all accounts across all organizations
  • 3 Deactivate: Prevent user from logging in
  • 4 Re-activate: Restore access
  • 5 Change Role: Update permissions (Owner/Admin/Manager/Sales)
5
System Health
  • 🖥️ Uptime: Server running time
  • 📦 Node Version: Runtime version
  • 💾 Memory: Current allocation
  • 🗄️ Database: Connectivity status
  • 📋 Error Feed: Recent errors (clearable)
6
Audit Trail
  • ✅ All admin actions are logged
  • 👤 Includes actor email and timestamp
  • 🔍 Written to affected org's audit log
  • 🔒 Cannot be deleted or modified
